SAP Solution Design How SAP S/4HANA + SAP Analytics Cloud solves RetailCo's three core problems
Problem 1 · Inventory
SAP Integrated Business Planning (IBP) connects demand forecasting with live stock data in real time — eliminating the 15% dead stock and €83K in annual stockout losses.
Problem 2 · Sales Visibility
SAP Analytics Cloud consolidates regional sales performance in real time — replacing month-end reporting lag with same-day decision intelligence across all five regions.
Problem 3 · Customer Insight
SAP Customer Experience (CX) + CRM enables RFM-driven personalisation — proactively targeting the 28% churn risk before customers are lost.
